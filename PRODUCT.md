# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Assessment developers, technical evaluators, and potential contributors exploring the QFlowLearn projects.

## Product purpose

A public project hub explaining Author, Deliver, the QTI library, and the AGPL v3 open-source release. Product evaluation and commercial information stay on qflowlearn.com.

## Brand personality

Precise, calm, evidence-led. Preserve the existing QFlowLearn identity.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic quiz-builder marketing, unsupported claims, and unsupported availability or certification claims.

## Design principles

Use explicit project boundaries and release statuses. Link to working public resources. Keep the content concise and available without JavaScript.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, strong contrast, and responsive reflow. No motion or interaction is required to read content.
