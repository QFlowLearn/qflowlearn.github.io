The projects

QFlow Author

Open source · AGPL-3.0-only

Create and review questions, organize question banks, import existing assessments, and export QTI packages.

Use and extend Author independently, including when you deliver assessments through another system. The source, license, and contribution guide are available on GitHub.

Browse Author source ↗

QFlow Deliver

Open source · AGPL-3.0-only

Publish assessments and manage learner attempts, timing, accommodations, results, and LMS connections.

Delivery has its own responsibilities: protecting learner work, coordinating attempts, and recording results. The delivery source is available under AGPL-3.0-only. Hosted services and support form part of the commercial offering.

Browse Deliver source ↗

Open source · MIT

QTI 3 library

A reusable TypeScript implementation for rendering, scoring, validating, and migrating QTI assessment content, with a Web Component player and optional React adapters.

Use it in your own application, independently of Author and Deliver.

What we’re working toward

Useful authoring, reusable standards tooling, and assessment content that can move between systems.

Independent authoring
Author should be useful when you deliver through another system. Creating and exporting an assessment should not require a QFlow Deliver account.
Explicit boundaries
Authoring, item rendering and scoring, and assessment delivery have distinct owners. The QTI library supports applications beyond QFlow.
Visible export changes
Preserve the original, produce a useful supported export, and disclose omitted or changed behavior. Give authors a concrete next step when content needs repair.

Follow the work

Author, Deliver, and the QTI library are public. Explore the code, read the contribution guides, or follow QFlowLearn for project updates.
